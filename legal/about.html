About QuickPDF

The private, no-upload PDF toolkit

Why QuickPDF exists

Most online PDF services require you to upload your documents to a stranger's server. For contracts, tax returns, medical records, or business documents, that's a real privacy problem — and it's slow.

QuickPDF flips that: your PDF is opened, edited, and saved entirely inside your web browser. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is stored, nothing is logged.

How it works technically

When you open a QuickPDF tool, we send you an HTML page plus a WebAssembly PDF engine (based on the excellent pdf-lib and PDF.js projects). When you pick a file, the browser reads it into memory, runs the operation locally, and hands you back a downloadable result. Our server never sees the file bytes.

The tradeoffs

  • Everything runs on your CPU, so massive PDFs on a slow phone may be sluggish. Desktops handle almost anything.
  • The first load of a tool downloads the engine (~2 MB). Subsequent loads are near-instant thanks to browser caching.
  • All conversion happens without OCR — text in scanned PDFs stays as an image.

Free forever

QuickPDF is funded by unobtrusive display ads on the landing page (never inside the tool workspace). We commit to keeping every current tool free with no watermarks, no signup, and no artificial limits.
