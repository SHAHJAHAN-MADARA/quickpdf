Privacy Policy

Last updated:

Summary

QuickPDF is designed so that we cannot see your files even if we wanted to. Every PDF operation happens locally in your browser using WebAssembly. Files are never uploaded to our servers.

What we collect

  • Nothing about your files. They stay on your device.
  • Basic analytics. Aggregate page views and browser type via privacy-friendly analytics — no cookies that identify you personally.
  • Ads (if enabled). If we display Google AdSense ads, Google may set cookies to serve relevant ads. See Google's ad policies. You can opt out at adssettings.google.com.

Cookies

QuickPDF itself does not set any tracking cookies. Third-party embeds (like advertising) may set their own cookies according to their policies.

Data retention

Because we never receive your files, there is nothing to retain, delete, or share.

Children's privacy

QuickPDF is intended for a general audience aged 13 and above.

Questions? Email us via the contact page.
