    How to merge PDF files

    1. Click the drop area above and select the PDFs you want to combine.
    2. Drag the files in the list to arrange them in the order you want.
    3. Click Merge PDFs & Download. The combined file will save to your device automatically.

    The merge tool uses pdf-lib running via WebAssembly in your browser. Your files are never uploaded to a server. There is no size limit beyond your device's memory, no watermarks, and no signup.
