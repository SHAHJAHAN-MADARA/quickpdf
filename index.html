Frequently asked questions

Is QuickPDF really free?

Yes. Every tool is completely free with no signup required. We don't limit file size, add watermarks, or gate features behind a paywall. If you find this useful, sharing helps us keep it that way.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. QuickPDF uses WebAssembly to run entirely in your browser. Your PDFs are read, processed, and saved locally on your device — nothing is transmitted, stored, or logged.

What's the maximum PDF size I can process?

There is no server-side limit. The practical ceiling depends on your device's RAM — most modern computers handle PDFs up to 500 MB comfortably. On mobile, ~100 MB is a safer target.

Do QuickPDF tools work offline?

After the page has loaded once, most tools work offline because the processing engine is cached by your browser. Load a tool once with internet, then reload it with connection off.

Which browsers are supported?

Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari (14+), and Brave all work. QuickPDF requires JavaScript and WebAssembly, which every mainstream browser supports.

Can I use QuickPDF for confidential documents?

Yes — that's the point. Because processing is local, QuickPDF is ideal for legal contracts, medical records, tax documents, and anything you wouldn't want on someone else's server.
